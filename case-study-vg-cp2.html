Soil treatment and quality control

As a Field Engineer in Cameron Parish, Louisiana, I managed Portland cement usage and stabilized-soil production for a six-month, $86 million scope.

The operation treated approximately 2.8 million cubic yards of soil. Field controls included cement usage verification, quality testing, production reporting, and schedule tracking.

Project requirements

Verify that soil treatment met project specifications, account for cement usage, and monitor production against the six-month schedule.

Responsibilities

I managed cement usage and production tracking and verified application rates, treatment depth, moisture conditioning, mixing, compaction, and density testing.

Field control

I directed corrective work when test results or field conditions fell outside project requirements and tracked the work in production and schedule reports.
