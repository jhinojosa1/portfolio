Prototype

Long-Range HaLow Field Network

This prototype uses Wi-Fi HaLow radios, Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W field nodes, and a Raspberry Pi 4 gateway to extend site office connectivity to remote equipment. Intended uses include survey data transfer, design file exchange, and equipment communication. Range depends on terrain, antenna placement, interference, and line of sight.

Houston Community Resilience Mesh

I am exploring a Houston-area Meshtastic network that would relay short messages over LoRa during cellular or internet outages. The proposed nodes would use solar power, battery backup, and weather-resistant enclosures. The network is intended to supplement established emergency communications.
